# ChatAudio 1.12

This update focuses on refining the 3D cassette experience and streamlining the recording UI.

### 3D Model Enhancements
- **Balanced Tape Visuals**: Added a matching tape coil to the right spool of the 3D cassette, making it look more realistic as tape transfers between reels.
- **Refined Aesthetics**: Removed the red recording dot from the 3D model for a cleaner, more integrated look.

### UI Improvements
- **Simplified History Sidebar**: The history header now remains static during dictation, and the recording card has been simplified to show a centered "Listening..." status, keeping the interface focused and distraction-free.
